01When to get in touch

Your website loads slowly

Pages wait on an overloaded shared server. A static website has nothing to compute — the file is served straight away.

The bill keeps growing and you are not sure what for

Hosting, domain, certificate, backups and maintenance, each billed separately. Send us what you pay and we will compare it for you.

Nobody knows who changed what

Files get overwritten over FTP and the latest version lives on someone's hard drive. With us, every change has an author, a timestamp and a way back.

Nobody has updated the website in ages

An old content management system and outdated plugins are the most common way websites get hacked. A static website has nothing to attack.

02How it works

  1. Sign-up

    A few details, nothing to install. Your account is used for invoicing and gives you an overview of what runs on your website.

  2. Domain

    We transfer it from any registrar — Czech ones such as Subreg (Gransy), Wedos, Banán and Forpsi, or registrars abroad — or register a new one for you. You pay only the domain registration fee; the HTTPS certificate is included.

  3. Migration or a new buildfree up to a certain size

    We move your existing website so that page addresses stay the same and search engines lose nothing. If you don't have a website or yours has had its day, we will build a new one.

  4. Operation

    Running the website, HTTPS, backups and uptime monitoring are on us. You send us the changes you want, or manage them yourself through Web Editor.

03Changes through Web Editor

Web Editor is our tool for pointing at an element of your website and saying what should happen to it. With hosting you get a connector for it, so an edit doesn't stop at a description: it goes straight into your website.

  • Preview first. Every change is deployed to its own preview address, where you can review it at your own pace. The live website stays untouched for now.
  • Then production. Once you approve the preview, the change goes live on its own. No manual deployment, no waiting for a developer.
  • There is always a way back. Every deployment is kept in the history. Rolling back to a previous version takes moments.

Web Editor is still in development and is being tested by invitation. Your hosting does not depend on it — it runs without it, and we make the changes for you. More about Web Editor.

04Business email

A domain comes with email. We set up mailboxes on your own domain, including the records that deliverability depends on — SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Without them, your messages end up in spam and anyone can send mail in your domain's name.

  • On your account. We create the mailboxes with a trusted provider; the contract and the data are yours. We just set them up and hand them over.
  • Or managed by us. Email runs as a service from us, and you sort out anything with a single email. We will go with whatever suits you.

We also handle newsletters and automated emails from your website — order confirmations, form replies, notifications.

06Frequently asked questions

What does “free up to a certain size” mean?

We move a typical business website of a handful to a few dozen pages at no charge. For large websites, or websites that first need to be extracted from an old system, we tell you the price up front — never after the fact.

How much does hosting cost?

We price it based on the size of your website and what you want with it. Send us your website address and what you pay for it today; you will get a comparison and a specific quote by email, free of charge and with no obligation.

Will we lose our search engine rankings?

No. We keep page addresses the same, and where that is not possible, we set up permanent redirects. Websites are usually faster after migration, which tends to help with search engines.

Do we need to know Git?

No. Git runs under the hood so that every change can be traced and undone. You don't have to work with it — if you want, we will give you access to the repository.

How quickly can a website be moved?

The move itself usually takes one to two days. The domain transfer at your current registrar typically takes longer — the whole time, your website keeps running at its old address, so there is no downtime to deal with.

What if we want to leave?

You get your website's content and its full history in the repository, and you transfer the domain wherever you like. Nothing keeps you with us except being happy with the service.
