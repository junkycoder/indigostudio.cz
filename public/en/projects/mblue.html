Case study · HR and recruitment

A custom CRM and recruitment system for the staffing agency mBlue

mBlue Czech recruits employees for corporate clients and provides HR consulting. For years, it worked in a system it had outgrown. We designed and developed an application in which the team manages sales, recruitment, timesheets and reporting in one place — and migrated the entire company’s historical data into it.

01Starting point

The agency managed clients, candidates and job orders in a general-purpose system it had used for many years. It no longer fit the way the agency worked:

  • A job order could not have more than one person assigned, even though recruitment is routinely a team effort.
  • There was no true deletion of personal data under GDPR with a record of who deleted it and when.
  • Sales, recruitment and invoicing lived in separate places — reports for management were put together by hand.

The goal was not another off-the-shelf application, but a system built precisely around how the agency sells, fills positions and evaluates results.

02What we built

One application for the whole company — for consultants, team leaders, back office and management. Work happens in a fast list–detail interface where changes take effect instantly, without reloading the page.

Candidate database

Full-text search that includes résumé contents, duplicate checks, tags and languages. An uploaded résumé pre-fills the form.

Clients and contacts

Company profiles with lookup in ARES (the Czech business register), industries, segments and holding relationships.

Sales pipeline

Acquisition and retention with pricing. A signed deal automatically creates a recruitment project.

Recruitment and hiring

Custom recruitment stages on top of a fixed reporting framework, a funnel, rejection reasons and start dates.

Pricing

Service pricing based on the catalog and commercial terms, with access limited to designated roles.

Timesheets and invoicing

Timesheets, exports, PDF invoices with QR payment codes, and a financial plan.

Documents and recordings

Text extraction from PDFs, office files and scans (OCR), and transcription of recordings.

Reports

Management, sales, accounting and delivery reports built on unified data.

03Historical data migration

Without its history, the new application would have been of no value to the agency. From the original system, we migrated clients, candidates, job orders and the progress of hiring processes. The client decided what to migrate and what to keep in the archive.

The migration is repeatable — running it again duplicates nothing — and it gives a reason for every record it skips. The client can see what was migrated and why, and the history is searchable in the new application from day one.

04Challenges and solutions

Reports that don’t lie

Problem
Every team names its recruitment stages its own way. Typical systems then cannot compare results across projects.
Solution
Custom stage names map to eight fixed reporting steps. When a candidate moves back a step, the history is not lost — the system distinguishes where the candidate is now from how far they have progressed.

Searching and sorting in Czech

Problem
A large database, diacritics, and names that must be sorted the way Czech requires.
Solution
Full-text search finds records by the beginnings of words, with or without diacritics. Czech sorting works throughout the application, and people are sorted by surname. We verified speed with load tests on 250,000 records, and automated tests guard the correctness of sorting.

A consistent and accessible interface

Problem
Dozens of screens where people spend their entire workday.
Solution
A custom design system with a live component catalog that renders the application’s actual production components. Automated tests check contrast, the size of controls and labels for screen readers.

Personal data under control

Problem
The agency manages sensitive personal data of candidates and client contacts.
Solution
Irreversible deletion of personal data with an audit record, passwordless sign-in via a one-time link, a content security policy, and outgoing email restricted to approved addresses.

Deliberately simple operations: background jobs without additional services, and no frontend framework. This keeps the application maintainable for the long term without rising infrastructure costs.

Quality is backed by more than 2,000 unit and integration tests and nearly 500 end-to-end browser scenarios, static security analysis, and separate environments for development, testing and production.

06Outcome

In five and a half months, we built an application that covers the agency’s entire cycle — from the sales opportunity through recruitment and timesheets to invoicing and management reporting. Historical data has been migrated and is searchable.

Since late August 2026, the client’s team has been testing the application, and development continues based on its feedback — currently management reports, publishing job ads to job portals and a new overview on the home screen.
