Taking over software development and maintenance

When your software supplier leaves

We take over development of your app or internal system from your previous supplier or developer. First we find out what state the software is in, and only then do you decide what comes next. The review before the takeover is free.

When to contact us

Your supplier has quit or stopped responding

The agency has closed, the freelancer has moved on to other work, or requests take weeks to get an answer.

Your in-house developer has left

The only person who knew the software is no longer with the company. Nobody knows what is in the code.

The system has reached the end of its life

Nobody wants to make changes to the old information system, and work is moving into spreadsheets alongside it.

The app cannot be released

The mobile app crashes, fails App Store or Google Play review, or has different code for each platform.

How the takeover works

  1. Reviewfree

    A one-hour meeting and a look into the code, servers and accounts, after signing an NDA if you request one. You receive a short written report: code condition, main risks, an estimate of takeover costs and what to do first.

  2. Access handover

    We list and take over everything the software depends on: the repository, servers, databases, domains, certificates, App Store and Google Play accounts, and third-party services. We verify that you have working backups.

  3. Stabilization

    We set up automated builds and releases, production error monitoring, and tests where regressions are most likely. We fix the bugs that hold you back the most.

  4. Further development or replacement

    Based on what we found in the code, we recommend whether to keep developing the software, rewrite it gradually or replace it. The decision is yours.

Frequently asked questions

Do you know the technology the software is written in?

Among others, we work with Vue.js, Ruby on Rails, Swift, Kotlin and hybrid apps built on Capacitor. If we cannot cover a technology ourselves or with our network of specialists, we will tell you at the review.

What if we have no access to the source code?

That can be solved too. At the review, we find out what you have available, where the code most likely is and how to get it. If that is not possible, we will propose a replacement.

How much does a takeover cost?

It depends on the scope and condition of the software. The review is free and includes a cost estimate, so you know the price before you commit to anything.

How soon can you start?

We reply to messages within a few hours. We schedule the review based on how urgent the situation is.

Do you take on every project?

No. Writing to us costs nothing and does not commit you to anything. However, we reserve the right to decline an inquiry if, from your message or the review, we judge that a takeover would not be worthwhile for you or for us. In that case, we will tell you right away.

Need someone to take over your software?

Tell us what software it is and what happened. We will get back to you within a few hours.
