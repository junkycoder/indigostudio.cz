Using the logo

  • Write the name as Indigo Studio, two words, both capitalized. The full company name is Indigo Studio s.r.o.
  • Use the Indigo logo on light backgrounds and the dark-background version on dark ones.
  • Leave clear space around the logo at least as tall as the crown.
  • Minimum size: 120 px wide for the horizontal logo, 24 px for the mark alone.
  • Please don’t alter the logo: no recoloring, stretching, shadows or effects.

About us

Indigo Studio s.r.o. is a family-run software studio run by Daniel and Veronika. We build web, iOS and Android apps and custom internal systems, host websites and train companies to work with AI tools.
