05Common questions

What if I don't like the design?

The design comes with no obligation. If you decide to keep your current website, you pay nothing.

What is included in hosting?

Running the website, HTTPS, backups and the changes you email us, such as new prices or opening hours. All of this costs CZK 5,082 per year including VAT.

Will my website keep working during the transfer?

Yes. It keeps running at the old address the whole time, so there is no downtime. We keep page addresses the same, and where that is not possible, we set up permanent redirects.

Does this work for an online store?

The offer is for presentation websites: introducing your business, prices, photos, contact details and opening hours. Online stores with inventory and payments are handled separately, and we are happy to advise.

What if I want to leave?

You get your website content and its full change history, and you transfer the domain elsewhere. Nothing holds you except being happy with the service.

Why only until December 23?

We want to give everyone personal attention and get everything done this year.
